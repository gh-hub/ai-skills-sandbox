# Glossary: Sticky header on scroll

## Hero card
The bordered, terminal-window-styled card titled "Thanks, Claude (code)" at the top of the home page (`apps/web/app/page.tsx`), containing a fake title bar (with `HeaderAuthControl` in it), a welcome panel, and a tips panel.

## Sticky header
The new fixed-position bar being built in this plan, which appears once the hero card scrolls out of view.
